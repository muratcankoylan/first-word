ITEM N09 - Hubble: a dense star field in Sagittarius (the "SWEEPS field" toward the galactic bulge), 4097 x 4097 px
WHAT: Wall-to-wall stars, white, blue and orange, with diffraction spikes on the bright ones; no black gaps, no nebula. Hubble Advanced Camera for Surveys. Full square frame, 4097x4097, master TIFF.
  NOTE: this is not the 1998 Hubble Heritage picture titled "Sagittarius Star Cloud" (the archive already has that at 1024 px; its existing rights record says the source TIFF is only 1024x1009, which I did not re-check). It is a different, much larger Hubble image of a crowded field in the same constellation.
HOLDER: NASA (science.nasa.gov Hubble asset library); produced by the Space Telescope Science Institute (STScI release 2011-16).
