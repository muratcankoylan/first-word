## What it is
It's a skill plus a Python CLI that works out how a video was made. You give it a reference video and, shot by shot, it tells you how each shot was produced and how to remake it. It's built for agents like Claude Code and Codex. The skill file is [skills/rea-video/SKILL.md](rea-video/skills/rea-video/SKILL.md), and it's written in Chinese.

## What it answers
1. **How each shot was made**, using 11 categories:
   - filmed for real, clips from film or TV, AI video, AI avatar
   - 3D render, 2D animation, stop-motion
   - code or template render (e.g. Remotion), screen recording
   - a still image with a pan or zoom, a frozen frame

   It also flags AI edits made on top of real footage: face swap, lip-sync, restyling, background replacement and beautify filters.
2. **What's layered on top:** captions with timings (exports to SRT/ASS), styled text, stickers, GIFs and picture-in-picture.
3. **Real material you'd have to supply yourself:** screenshots, screen recordings, product photos.
4. **Audio:**
   - It separates voice from music.
   - It judges whether the voice is TTS or a real person, and whether the music is AI or library music.
   - It measures BPM and finds where sound effects line up with on-screen events.
5. **For filmed footage:** framing, camera position, lighting setup with a gear list, color grade and cutting rhythm.
6. **A remake plan:** what to shoot, what to generate (through a separate tool called capsule-cinema, which charges per run) and what to rebuild in code with Remotion.

## How it decides
- **Measurements first:** it checks things like sensor noise, the duplicate frames left when 24fps footage is turned into 30fps (a sign of AI or film sources), how many colors a frame uses, flat areas, text read from the frame, and metadata tags that mark AI-generated content.
- **Optional vision model:** you can connect a model that can see video, like Gemini or Doubao, through an OpenAI-style API.
- **Agent review:** if the agent can see images, it's told to always check the shots itself with `review`, fill in its answers, then run `apply-review`. Its answers are combined with the measurements rather than replacing them. The repo's own tests say this beats Gemini on its own.
- **Honesty rule:** every conclusion needs evidence. If it's unsure, it says "to be confirmed" with a leaning (for example "leaning AI, 70%") instead of guessing.

## Main commands
`decompose` → `summary` → `review` / `apply-review` → `remake-plan` / `remake-run` (paid, always try `--dry-run` first) → `assemble`. There are also `tiles`, `stems`, `memory`, `correct` and `prefs` for remembering your corrections and preferences. Reports come out in Chinese or English (`--lang`).
