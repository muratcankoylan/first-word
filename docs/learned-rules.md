# Rules learned from review

During the production the agent kept a short list of standing rules. Each one was written after a correction from
human review, with the date, what prompted it and what to do differently, and each was loaded into every later
context window. This is how a correction made once stayed made after the agent's context was replaced.

They are given here in the order they were learned. The triggers are summarised.

| # | Learned | Trigger | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 10 Nov | A research subagent's summary hedged on facts that its own saved captures contained | When a summary is thin or hedged on something that matters, read the raw output it saved. Ask every subagent to say where its raw output is. |
| 2 | 10 Nov | The plan was approved with creative decisions delegated | Decide creative and production questions and record the reason. Return to the director only for credentials, spending beyond the cap, and rights or reputation risks, each stated as a default that will be followed unless they object. |
| 3 | 10 Nov | The first voices, designed from text and picked by measurement, sounded synthetic | Read the vendor's own guidance before generating audio. Cast voices from real recordings. Generate dialogue as whole scenes so voices answer each other. Let the cut follow the performance. Always give a person a page to listen on, and say that nothing was judged by ear. |
| 4 | 10 Nov | The full-size pass was held back in review until the cut was finished (a 1080p finish costs about 3.4 times a draft per second) | Work in drafts until the cut is locked. Finishing at full size is a spending decision and needs a go-ahead. A fault inside one person's take is listed, not rerolled, because a reroll changes the face. |
| 5 | 11 Nov | An audit found plan rows reworded to match what had been built, and planned sounds missing | Audit against the plan as approved, on rendered frames and measured sound, not against the agent's own notes. Check the sound and transition columns as well as the picture. Never reword a plan row to make a gap disappear. |
| 6 | 11 Nov | Flat rectangles drawn in code looked like placeholders beside generated footage | Nothing flat-filled goes on screen. Anything drawn has a core, a glow and motion, and belongs to one light system. No shot is a still. Real archive gets its scanline treatment and nothing else. Lines spoken on camera are locked to the cast's mouths; only voice-over and on-screen text may be reworded. |
| 7 | 11 Nov | The score measured as sub bass; effects landed late; voices clipped in the loudest passage | Before music enters a mix, map it bar by bar in four frequency bands and discard a take that is only bass. Place every hit by its sharpest moment and check it against its frame. Master with a limiter. Never synthesise a sound, even as a stopgap. |
| 8 | 11 Nov | Four rounds of review found on-screen text that passed too fast or needed explaining | Run the reading-load report on every render and treat "freeze-frame only" as a fault unless it is deliberate texture. Read each line as a stranger would, and cut any word that only makes sense from inside the production. When time is too short for the words, say so with the numbers. |
| 9 | 11 Nov | Decisions about the last screen were made in review | Decisions made in review are recorded and not reopened when the cut is audited against the older plan. |
| 10 | 11 Nov | The first process images were dark boards with large headlines | Explanatory figures are set as figures in a paper: white page, lettered panels, tables with three rules, charts with axes, a caption underneath, and the finding in the caption. |
| 11 | 11 Nov | The agent stated that an earlier artifact did not exist, having searched one of two session records | Search the whole record before stating what was or was not made. |

## What these have in common

Most of these correct a judgment the agent made from a proxy: a measurement standing in for an ear (3, 7), its own
notes standing in for the screen (5), a summary standing in for the source (1), one search standing in for the
whole record (11), its own reading standing in for a first-time viewer's (8). The remedy each time was to go back
to the primary thing (the audio in front of a listener, the rendered frame, the raw capture, the plan as approved)
and to write the rule down where the next context window would read it.
