ITEM N01 - Langley Aerodrome, first launch failure, Potomac River, 7 October 1903 - Plate 97
WHAT: Two photographs on one plate: workmen hauling the wrecked Aerodrome out of the water at the houseboat; and the wreck lying on the raft beside the houseboat.
  Printed caption on the plate: "AERODROME BEING RECOVERED, OCTOBER 7, 1903".
  The plate is from: S. P. Langley and Charles M. Manly, "Langley Memoir on Mechanical Flight", Smithsonian Contributions to Knowledge vol. 27 no. 3 (Washington: Smithsonian Institution, 1911). The printed running head on every plate reads "SMITHSONIAN CONTRIBUTIONS TO KNOWLEDGE  VOL. 27, NO. 3, PL. <n>".
  The memoir text says: "Plate 97, Fig. 1, which shows the aerodrome being hoisted from the water, and in Plate 97, Fig. 2, which shows it just afterwards resting on the raft, the wings, tail and rudder having been completely demolished by towing it through the water".
