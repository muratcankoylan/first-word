ITEM N08 - Ingenuity's first flight, 19 April 2021: the navigation-camera frames with the helicopter's own shadow
READ THIS FIRST: I could not find any NASA-published VIDEO of the first flight from the navigation camera. What NASA holds and publishes for that flight is 15 still frames from the downward-looking black-and-white navigation camera (Sol 58). I saved all 15 as NASA serves them, and I also joined them into one clip MYSELF so they can be dropped on a timeline. That clip is my assembly, not a NASA release. (A real NASA navcam shadow video exists for Flight 14; it is saved as the N08 alternate.)
WHAT: 15 frames, 640x480, black and white. f01-f03: the shadow on the ground shrinking as the helicopter climbs; f04-f09: hover, small shadow near centre over banded, pebbly ground; f10-f12: descent, shadow growing; f13-f15: on the ground, the shadow of body and legs filling the frame.

WHAT I CHECKED TO BE SURE THERE IS NO OFFICIAL FIRST-FLIGHT NAVCAM VIDEO:
  - NASA Image and Video Library searches ("ingenuity navigation camera", "ingenuity first flight", video): nothing for flight one from this camera.
  - JPL Photojournal entries PIA24584 to PIA24593: 24584 is the single still (already in the archive as G12); 24586 is the rover's Navcam; 24588 is a data animation; 24589 and 24590 are Mastcam-Z; 24592 is flight two. None is a flight-one navigation-camera video.
  - JPL's 2021 and 2023 Ingenuity media reels in the NASA library: in the 2021 reel (49 minutes; I sampled one frame every 10 seconds, so a very short insert could be missed) the navigation-camera picture appears only on control-room screens; the 2023 reel's navigation-camera sequences are slated as flights 14 and 25.
  This is a negative result from searching, not a statement from NASA that no such video exists.
