ITEM N08 (alternate) - a real NASA navigation-camera video of Ingenuity's shadow: FLIGHT 14, 24 October 2021 - NOT the first flight
READ THIS FIRST: this is not 19 April 2021. It is a later flight. It is here because it is an actual NASA-made moving sequence of the shadow taking off, hovering and landing, which does not exist for flight one. Do not caption it as the first flight.
