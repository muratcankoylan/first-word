ITEM N01 - Langley Aerodrome, first launch failure, Potomac River, 7 October 1903 - Plate 96
WHAT: The Aerodrome photographed from directly underneath as it left the houseboat: wings, frame and a spinning propeller against the sky. Plate also carries the line "PHOTOGRAPH BY T. W. SMILLIE".
  Printed caption on the plate: "FLIGHT OF LARGE AERODROME, OCTOBER 7, 1903".
  The plate is from: S. P. Langley and Charles M. Manly, "Langley Memoir on Mechanical Flight", Smithsonian Contributions to Knowledge vol. 27 no. 3 (Washington: Smithsonian Institution, 1911). The printed running head on every plate reads "SMITHSONIAN CONTRIBUTIONS TO KNOWLEDGE  VOL. 27, NO. 3, PL. <n>".
  The memoir text says of it: "Plate 96, which was taken at the same instant as the one just described ... is a view of the machine as it passed almost directly over Mr. Smillie's head".
