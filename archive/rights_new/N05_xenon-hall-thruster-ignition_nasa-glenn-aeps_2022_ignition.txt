ITEM N05 - an electric (xenon) thruster igniting in a vacuum chamber at NASA Glenn, 2022 - real test-camera footage, 4K
WHAT: A fixed test camera looking at the face of a thruster inside a vacuum chamber (gold thermal blanket and cabling around it). The cathode at the centre glows orange; then the annular channel lights and the whole face floods with blue-cyan glow, which holds to the end of the clip. Complete clip as NASA published it, 5.6 seconds, 3840x2160. A small burned-in clock ("13:54:47") sits in the top-left corner and there is a thin black band along the top edge.
  IMPORTANT - what kind of engine: NASA's record tags it "aeps" / "advanced electric propulsion system" (the Gateway Power and Propulsion Element thruster). AEPS is a Hall-effect thruster. It is NOT one of the gridded ion engines the brief named (NSTAR, NEXT, Dawn, DS1). That AEPS is a Hall thruster running on xenon comes from NASA's other AEPS pages and papers, not from this record, which does not say "xenon" or "Hall".
