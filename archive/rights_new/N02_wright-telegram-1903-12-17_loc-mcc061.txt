ITEM N02 - Orville Wright's telegram to Bishop Milton Wright, Kitty Hawk, 17 December 1903 (colour scan, high resolution)
WHAT: The Western Union telegram form, typed: "Success four flights thursday morning all against twenty one mile wind started from Level with engine power alone average speed through air thirty one miles longest 57 seconds inform Press home [struck out] Christmas . Orevelle Wright 525P". Colour scan of the whole sheet.
HOLDER: Library of Congress, Manuscript Division (item mcc.061, in the Library's online selection "Words and Deeds in American History"; "Source Collection: Wright Brothers Papers").

CAVEATS:
  1. INFERRED: the mcc.061 page has no rights statement. I rely on the Library's statement for the Wright Papers, which is the collection this telegram belongs to (the item page names it as source collection, and its negative number LC-MSS-46706-5 carries the Wright Papers collection number 46706). The Library shows the same telegram inside the Wright Papers at https://www.loc.gov/resource/mss46706.02039/?sp=34 with that statement printed on the page; that lower-resolution scan is saved as the N02 alternate. If the stricter reading is wanted, use the alternate.
  2. Supporting, INFERRED not quoted: the text is Orville Wright's (died 1948); the printed blank is a Western Union form of 1903.
  3. The Library "makes no warranty" and leaves the legal assessment to the user (quoted above).
  4. A separate Prints & Photographs record of a black-and-white copy negative (https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/2003680165/) says "Rights Advisory: Rights status not evaluated." That file was NOT taken.
  5. No people. Names on the sheet: Orville and Milton Wright; Robert C. Clowry (printed, Western Union president).
