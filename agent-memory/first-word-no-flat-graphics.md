On 11 November 2026, after watching animatic v009 of First Word, the user wrote: "I really don't like the blue square or the blue continued graphic. I think we need to improve that and make it more futuristic and more dynamic. Also, I want you to review the entire movie again and improve some of the scripts, some of the transcripts, and some of the transitions, and increase the dynamism and futuristicism."

They did not say which graphic they meant. My reading: every flat cyan rectangle the film drew itself (the cursor block, the block over the bleeped word, the square that wiped the wall, the bar that shrank into the cursor at the end, and the square holding the place of the maker's mark on the end card). I replaced all of them in v010 with one system drawn as light (tools/light.py).

**Why:** the user gave no reason beyond "more futuristic and more dynamic". My reading: flat fills looked like placeholders next to generated footage.

**How to apply:** never put a flat-filled shape on screen in this film. Anything drawn gets a core, a glow and motion, and belongs to the same light as the tube lights and the baton. Stamps and cards land; they do not just appear. No shot should be a still. Do not add effects to real archive beyond its scanline tube. When changing wording, only voice-over and on-screen text are free: lines spoken on camera are locked to the cast's mouths. See [[first-word-film-project]] and [[do-not-game-the-plan]].
