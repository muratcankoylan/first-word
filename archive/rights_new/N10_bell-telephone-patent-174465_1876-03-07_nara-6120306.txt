ITEM N10 - Alexander Graham Bell's patent drawing, U.S. Patent No. 174,465, "Telegraphy", patented 7 March 1876 (the telephone patent) - both sheets
WHAT: The two original drawing sheets from the Patent Office file, colour scans. Sheet 1: Figs. 1-5 (dash diagrams, undulating-current curves, two electromagnets on a line), a small printed clipping pasted at the right edge, stamps "CLASSIFICATION DIVISION" and "11357". Sheet 2: Fig. 6 (three groups of instruments on one circuit) and Fig. 7 (the speaking-telephone arrangement: cone, membrane, armature, electromagnet, line, receiver). Each sheet is headed "A. G. BELL. TELEGRAPHY. No. 174,465. Patented March 7, 1876." and signed for the inventor and by two witnesses.
HOLDER: U.S. National Archives and Records Administration, NAID 6120306 (Record Group 241, Records of the Patent and Trademark Office; Series: Utility Patent Drawings; National Archives at College Park - Cartographic)
