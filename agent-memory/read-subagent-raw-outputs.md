When a research subagent hands back a summary, check the raw outputs it saved (search and extract results in its scratchpad folder) before using or dismissing its findings.

**Why:** on 2026-11-10 a researcher's report on Seedance 2.5 marked several items "could not verify" although its saved captures held Runway's full prompt guide, help article and API guide. The user could see those outputs and told me to retrieve them properly. Reading them changed the generation plan (prompt structure, no face references, audio-bound lip sync, 24 fps, costs).

**How to apply:** ask subagents to say where raw outputs are saved; when a report is vague or hedged on something that matters, open the captured pages directly and mine them with targeted searches rather than re-running the research. Related: [[first-word-film-project]].
