ITEM N04 - Hurricane Dorian with a clear eye, seen moving from the International Space Station, 1 September 2019
WHAT: Real-time view from a camera outside the station as it passes the storm: the whole cloud shield with the eye open near the centre of frame for the full 30 seconds, ocean and turquoise shallows at upper left (NASA's caption places the storm over the northern Bahamas), Earth's limb at the bottom. The motion is the station's travel; the clouds do not visibly rotate at real-time speed.
