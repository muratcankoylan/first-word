ITEM N01 - Langley Aerodrome, first launch failure, Potomac River, 7 October 1903 - Plate 100
WHAT: Two photographs on one plate: the wreck in the water with tug-boats and rowing boats; and the wreck, wings crumpled, beside a tug.
  Printed caption on the plate: "AERODROME IN WATER, OCTOBER 7, 1903".
  The plate is from: S. P. Langley and Charles M. Manly, "Langley Memoir on Mechanical Flight", Smithsonian Contributions to Knowledge vol. 27 no. 3 (Washington: Smithsonian Institution, 1911). The printed running head on every plate reads "SMITHSONIAN CONTRIBUTIONS TO KNOWLEDGE  VOL. 27, NO. 3, PL. <n>".
  The memoir text says: "Plates 98 to 100 show the aerodrome in the water from the moment after it arose and the writer, who had extricated himself while it was plunging down through the water ... until the machine was taken in tow by the tug-boat."
