ITEM N01 - Langley Aerodrome, first launch failure, Potomac River, 7 October 1903 - Plate 95
WHAT: The Aerodrome nose-down in the air just off the front of the launching houseboat, an instant after leaving the track. This is the picture of the 7 October 1903 failure.
  Printed caption on the plate: "FLIGHT OF LARGE AERODROME, OCTOBER 7, 1903".
  The plate is from: S. P. Langley and Charles M. Manly, "Langley Memoir on Mechanical Flight", Smithsonian Contributions to Knowledge vol. 27 no. 3 (Washington: Smithsonian Institution, 1911). The printed running head on every plate reads "SMITHSONIAN CONTRIBUTIONS TO KNOWLEDGE  VOL. 27, NO. 3, PL. <n>".
  The memoir text (same scan, read in its OCR text) says: "Referring to the photograph, Plate 95, which was taken by Mr. G. H. Powell, Secretary of the Board of Ordnance and Fortification, and which shows the machine just a few feet in front of the point where it was actually launched ..."
